# 🖥️ How to Open Terminal - Step by Step

Don't worry! Opening the terminal is easy. Here's how to do it on your computer:

## 🪟 **Windows**

### Method 1: Search Bar (Easiest)
1. Click the **Start button** (Windows logo in bottom-left corner)
2. Type: `cmd` or `command prompt`
3. Click on **Command Prompt** when it appears
4. A black window will open - this is your terminal!

### Method 2: Keyboard Shortcut
1. Press `Windows Key + R` together
2. Type: `cmd`
3. Press Enter

### Method 3: Right-click (Windows 11)
1. Right-click on the **Start button**
2. Click **Terminal** or **Windows Terminal**

---

## 🍎 **Mac**

### Method 1: Spotlight Search (Easiest)
1. Press `Command (⌘) + Spacebar` together
2. Type: `terminal`
3. Press Enter when you see **Terminal.app**
4. A window will open - this is your terminal!

### Method 2: Applications Folder
1. Open **Finder**
2. Click **Applications** in the left sidebar
3. Open the **Utilities** folder
4. Double-click **Terminal**

### Method 3: Launchpad
1. Open **Launchpad** (rocket icon in your dock)
2. Type: `terminal` in the search
3. Click the **Terminal** icon

---

## 🐧 **Linux**

### Method 1: Keyboard Shortcut (Most Common)
1. Press `Ctrl + Alt + T` together
2. Terminal opens automatically!

### Method 2: Applications Menu
1. Click **Applications** or **Show Applications**
2. Search for: `terminal`
3. Click the Terminal app

---

## ✅ **What You'll See**

When the terminal opens, you'll see a window with text that looks something like:

**Windows:**
```
C:\Users\YourName>
```

**Mac/Linux:**
```
YourName@computer:~$
```

This is normal! It's waiting for you to type commands.

---

## 📝 **What to Do Next**

Once your terminal is open:

### **For Windows:**
1. Navigate to your TEALHOUSE project folder
2. Type: `deploy.bat` and press Enter
3. Follow the prompts!

### **For Mac/Linux:**
1. Navigate to your TEALHOUSE project folder
2. Type: `chmod +x deploy.sh` and press Enter
3. Type: `./deploy.sh` and press Enter
4. Follow the prompts!

---

## 🤔 **How to Navigate to Your Project Folder**

If your TEALHOUSE project is in a specific folder, you need to navigate there first:

### Windows:
```bash
cd C:\path\to\your\tealhouse\project
```

### Mac/Linux:
```bash
cd /path/to/your/tealhouse/project
```

**Tip:** You can usually drag and drop the folder onto the terminal window and it will auto-fill the path!
